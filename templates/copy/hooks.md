# Hook patterns

The hook is scene 1 / slide 1. It must land in ≤ 1.5 s of speech or ≤ 10 words on a slide.
Fill the brackets with the brand's audience language (brand.json → audience pains/desires).

| Pattern | Template | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Call-out | "If you [specific situation], watch this." | "If your coffee tastes bitter, watch this." |
| Mistake | "Stop [common action]." / "You're [doing X] wrong." | "Stop storing beans in the fridge." |
| Secret / insider | "[Pros] don't tell you this about [topic]." | "Baristas don't tell you this about grind size." |
| Number | "[N] [things] that [result]." | "3 changes that make home coffee taste café-good." |
| Before/after | "From [bad state] to [good state] in [time]." | "From bitter to sweet in one grind setting." |
| Myth | "[Common belief]? Wrong." | "Darker roast means stronger coffee? Wrong." |
| Curiosity gap | "This [small thing] changed my [outcome]." | "This $5 scale changed my morning coffee." |
| Direct promise | "Here's how to [desired result] without [pain]." | "How to get café espresso without a café machine." |
| POV / story | "POV: you finally [desired moment]." | "POV: you finally nailed latte art." |
| Question | "Why does [frustrating thing] happen?" | "Why does your coffee go stale so fast?" |
| Contrarian | "Unpopular opinion: [take]." | "Unpopular opinion: you don't need an espresso machine." |
| Proof-first | "[Impressive result]. Here's how." | "1,200 five-star reviews. Here's what we do differently." |

On-screen version of the hook ≤ 7 words; the spoken version can be a full sentence.
Avoid: "Hey guys", "In this video", "Did you know" (unless it's a genuinely surprising fact), clickbait the video doesn't pay off.
